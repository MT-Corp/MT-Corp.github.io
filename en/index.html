Before you start

Do I need an account?

Atelier has no account registration or sign-in. Your projects are managed locally.

Does everything work offline?

Document creation and processing run on your device. Ads, consent services and external links need a connection; rewarded ads depend on availability.

Are OCR and conversions always accurate?

No. Recognition may fail with handwriting, blurred images or unsupported scripts. Conversions may change fonts, positioning and complex elements.

Does the PPTX reader play animations?

It provides static viewing and presentation. It does not promise to preserve animations, transitions, multimedia or full PowerPoint or Canva compatibility.

What is in development?

A Spanish, English and French app interface is in development. Our own AI remains a research project and is not integrated as a generative assistant. No release date has been announced for these improvements.

Need a hand?

Email your device model, Atelier version and the steps that led to the problem. Avoid attaching personal documents or passwords.
